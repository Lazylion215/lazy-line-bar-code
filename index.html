Good to Know

We're a dry hire. Here's what that means.

Pennsylvania Law
Do you provide the alcohol?

Under Pennsylvania law, the host is responsible for purchasing all alcohol — we don't supply it. But you're not on your own. We work with a trusted third-party company that can pick up and deliver your alcohol directly to your venue. And we'll make sure you order exactly the right amount — nothing short, nothing wasted.
