Font Do's & Don'ts
✓Use DM Serif Display for all large headlines
✓Use Lora for body copy, labels, buttons
✓Use italic DM Serif for emphasis & brand voice
✗Don't mix too many font sizes
✗Don't use Arial, Roboto, or system fonts
✗Don't bold DM Serif Display — weight 400 is correct
04 · Brand Voice

Voice, Tone & Messaging

Use These Words & Phrases
  • Elevated experience
  • Crafted cocktails
  • Thoughtfully designed
  • Professional service
  • Host with confidence
  • Seamless event experience
  • Curated cocktail menu
  • Premium presentation
  • From setup to last call

Avoid These Words & Phrases
  • Cheap
  • Budget
  • Affordable
  • Lowest prices
  • Party bar
  • "We just pour drinks"
  • Generic mobile bartending language
  • Heavy black layouts that feel harsh
  • Clutter or busy design
  • Discount service tone
