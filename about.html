The Lazy Lion is a mobile bartending service for weddings, corporate & private events in Philadelphia and the surrounding areas. Founded in 2018 by Vincent Smith.

Request a Quote Our Story
No. 01 · The Origin

How we got started.

Lazy Lion Mobile Bar was founded with the belief that mobile bartending should feel like part of the event experience — not just a bar showing up to pour drinks.

We wanted to create something more elevated: handcrafted cocktails tailored to the celebration, bar setups that enhance the atmosphere, and professional hospitality that allows hosts to truly enjoy their event.

Today, Lazy Lion proudly serves Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs, bringing polished mobile bartending experiences to weddings, private parties, corporate events, and special celebrations.

From custom cocktails to thoughtful presentation, every detail is designed to create an experience guests remember long after the last drink is served.

No. 02 · Meet the Founder
Founder

Vincent Smith

Bar Director · Cocktail Program · Event Operations

Vince leads the Lazy Lion bar program — designing custom cocktail menus for each event, developing efficient batching and prep systems, and training the team to deliver the elevated standard Lazy Lion is known for.

With years of experience in hospitality and beverage operations, he understands the rhythm and flow required to deliver fast, seamless service without sacrificing quality or presentation.

The custom-built bars he designed and built by hand are an extension of that philosophy — thoughtfully crafted to balance both efficiency and aesthetics, creating a bar experience that looks as polished as it performs.

"Good Drinks. Good Vibes. Every single time."
No. 03 · How We Work

The Lazy Lion Standard.

"We believe mobile bartending should feel seamless, elevated, and intentional — from the first guest arrival to the final pour."

01

Designed, Not Generic.

No copied cocktail menus. No one-size-fits-all service.

Every event receives a tailored bar experience designed around your atmosphere, guest count, aesthetic, and style of celebration.

02

Hospitality First.

Great bartending is more than making drinks.

Our team is trained to create a calm, polished guest experience that feels professional, welcoming, and effortless throughout the event.

03

The Bar Becomes Part of the Atmosphere.

We believe the bar should contribute to the look and energy of the event — not feel separate from it.

From custom bar setups to curated garnishes and presentation, every detail is designed to elevate the space.

04

Prepared Behind the Scenes.

A smooth event starts long before guests arrive.

From alcohol guidance and batching preparation to setup flow and service planning, we prepare extensively so your event feels organized, relaxed, and stress-free.
